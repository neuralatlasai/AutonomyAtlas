# Publication format and venue boundary

Checked 29 October 2026. The complete work is a technical monograph, using a fixed book layout and IEEE numeric bibliography style. Citation style is not a claim of journal-template or venue compliance.

The [IEEE Computer Society author guidance](https://www.computer.org/publications/author-resources) states that submission length limits vary by journal and include references and author biographies. Its generic 12-page Transactions rule concerns the regular-paper overlength charge; the same guidance explicitly distinguishes submission limits. It must not be applied as a universal survey limit. A 360-plus-page monograph needs a separately scoped journal derivative if journal submission is selected.

The official [ACM Computing Surveys author-guidelines page](https://dl.acm.org/journal/csur/author-guidelines) returned HTTP403 during this check. No current ACM length limit is inferred from secondary summaries. No specific journal or conference has been selected, and no derivative is labeled submission compliant.

The [IEEE AI-content policy](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/become-an-ieee-journal-author/publishing-ethics/guidelines-and-policies/submission-and-peer-review-policies/) requires identification of AI systems and the scope of generated content in acknowledgments. The monograph identifies Codex assistance throughout its chapters and front matter, and image-generation assistance for all explanatory figures. The image tool did not expose its exact model identifier. No copied source figure or numerical result image is used. Human authorship, affiliations, permissions, final policy checks, and venue-specific disclosure wording must be established for an actual submission.
