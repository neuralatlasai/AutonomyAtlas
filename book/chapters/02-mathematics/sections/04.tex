\section{Optimization and policy-gradient identities}\label{sec:C02:04}
For supervised trajectories $(c,y)$, the negative log-likelihood objective is
\begin{equation}
 L_{\rm SFT}(\theta)=-\mathbb E\sum_i
 \log p_\theta(y_i\mid c,y_{<i}).
\end{equation}
The student input and supervised target must define a declared learning task. Expert targets can be constructed using privileged information; this is not intrinsically invalid supervision, although the student may face irreducible uncertainty. A stronger requirement applies when the loss is interpreted as a rollout-conditioned likelihood or gradient: every scored decision must use the information that actually conditioned behavior. MemoryWalker makes that distinction concrete for context editing~\cite{j2026memorywalker}. Offline relabeling may intentionally define another conditional task, but it cannot then be described as estimating the same behavior process.

For a differentiable deployed stochastic policy $\pi_{\theta,\delta}$, fixed decoding configuration $\delta$, finite horizon and return $R(\tau)$, define $J(\theta)=\mathbb E_{\tau\sim p_\theta}R(\tau)$. Then
\begin{align}
 \nabla_\theta J(\theta)
 &=\nabla_\theta\sum_\tau p_\theta(\tau)R(\tau)\\
 &=\mathbb E_{\tau\sim p_\theta}
 \left[R(\tau)\sum_t\nabla_\theta
 \log\pi_{\theta,\delta}(a_t\mid h_t)\right].
\end{align}
This score-function identity~\cite{ch02_policygradient1999} assumes that the environment dynamics and reward do not depend on $\theta$ through an unaccounted path, and that differentiation can pass through the expectation. If a learned reward or context policy shares parameters with the actor, the intended objective and gradient paths must be specified. Treating all feedback as a fixed scalar can silently omit dependencies. The probability law must be differentiable on the relevant support. A greedy decoder induces a Dirac action law; substituting raw softmax scores into its score-function identity generally estimates a different objective. Baseline fitting is detached from the actor score term unless the intended gradient explicitly includes that dependency.

A baseline $b(h_t)$ can reduce variance without changing the expected gradient because
\begin{equation}
 \mathbb E_{a\sim\pi_{\theta,\delta}(\cdot\mid h)}
 [b(h)\nabla_\theta\log\pi_{\theta,\delta}(a\mid h)]
 =b(h)\nabla_\theta\sum_a\pi_{\theta,\delta}(a\mid h)=0.
\end{equation}
An action-dependent baseline requires a correction; otherwise it can bias the estimate. Advantage estimators, clipping, group normalization, and KL penalties trade variance, bias, and stability under different assumptions. PPO and preference-optimization objectives are useful concrete examples, but they do not remove the need to preserve behavior conditioning and reward identity~\cite{schulman2017ppo,rafailov2023dpo}.

In general, importance sampling requires the target trajectory law to be absolutely continuous with respect to the behavior law, and weights by their Radon--Nikodym derivative. The following action-product specialization applies only when all nonpolicy kernels and the recorded information semantics are shared. For data generated by behavior policy $\mu$, it becomes
\begin{equation}
 w(\tau)=\prod_t
 \frac{\pi_{\theta,\delta}(a_t\mid h_t)}{\mu(a_t\mid h_t)}.
\end{equation}
The ratio is meaningful only when the behavior probability is known and the target distribution has support within the behavior distribution. A changed parser, retry controller, tool transition or authorization mechanism need not cancel from that trajectory ratio. A tool action made impossible by the old authorization policy cannot be recovered through a finite importance weight. Likewise, an unknown context-editing history can make the denominator undefined for the recorded action. Clipping large ratios can stabilize estimation but introduces a different estimator; it does not reconstruct missing information.


Agent Lightning makes this boundary inspectable. \path{RolloutAdapter.get_train_data_batch} in \path{agentlightning/verl/rollout_adapter.py} merges compatible token prefixes, masks inserted observations out of action loss, and omits batch \texttt{rollout\_log\_probs} if any row lacks usable captured values. \path{AgentLightningRayPPOTrainer._train_step} in \path{agentlightning/verl/trainer.py} requires finite captured log-probabilities in bypass mode; ordinary correction runs only when configured and the field is present~\cite{ch01_lightning_code2026}. These checks expose missing evidence. They do not establish equality between token probabilities and the action law after parsing, retries and authorization, or coverage of target-policy support.
