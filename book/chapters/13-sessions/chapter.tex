The 2026 ACM implementation exposes a consequential boundary between its live conversation, retained raw history, and checkpoint interface~\cite{repo_acm2026}. Continuity therefore depends on what a snapshot actually restores, rather than on the existence of a session identifier. That boundary connects model context directly to event ordering, external-effect reconciliation, concurrency, and retention semantics.
